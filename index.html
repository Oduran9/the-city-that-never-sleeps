THE QUESTION BEHIND THE NUMBERS

The city never sleeps.
Who ever gets to rest?

New York's energy is part of its identity. Its 311 records offer another story: moments when residents decided the sound around them warranted a 311 call.

These data can help us ask when complaints rise, where people report them, and what kinds of noise they describe. They cannot tell us how loud each block truly is or how many people experience noise without ever calling 311.

A complaint is a signal but it isnt the entire story.
